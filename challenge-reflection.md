## ✅ What Worked Well | 什么做得好

### Successful Strategies | 成功的策略

**What was our initial strategy? | 我们最初的策略是什么？**

Our initial plan was to build a wide triangular base for stability, then create a central tower with diagonal supports. We wanted to use the marshmallows as joints and keep the spaghetti pieces as long as possible to maximize height.

**What worked better than expected? | 什么比预期的效果更好？**

The triangular base concept was actually quite stable initially. Also, breaking some spaghetti pieces into shorter segments for reinforcement worked better than we thought - it gave us more flexibility in connecting pieces.

**What would you do the same way if you did it again? | 如果再做一次，你会用同样的方法做什么？**

I would definitely keep the idea of starting with a solid base before building up. The collaborative brainstorming at the beginning was also valuable - we should maintain that open discussion approach.



---

## ❌ What Failed | 什么失败了

### Challenges We Faced | 我们面临的挑战

**What didn't work as planned? | 什么没有按计划进行？**

Our structure kept collapsing when we tried to add height. The marshmallows weren't strong enough to hold the longer spaghetti pieces, and we spent too much time rebuilding instead of testing smaller sections first.

**Why do you think it failed? | 你认为为什么失败了？**

We focused too much on height and not enough on stability. The base wasn't wide enough, and we didn't reinforce the connections properly. Also, we should have tested the structure incrementally instead of building the whole thing and then testing.

**What was the biggest challenge? | 最大的挑战是什么？**

The biggest challenge was balancing the desire for height with the need for stability. Every time we tried to make it taller, it became more unstable. We also struggled with time pressure - we kept wanting to start over but didn't have enough time.

## 🔄 Iterations | 迭代

### How We Adapted | 我们如何适应

**How many different designs did we try? | 我们尝试了多少种不同的设计？**

☐ 1  ☑ 2-3  ☐ 4-5  ☐ 6+

**Describe one major change we made during the challenge | 描述我们在挑战中做出的一个重大改变**

After our first design collapsed, we completely changed our approach. Instead of one central tower, we tried building four corner supports connected at the top. This was more stable but took too long to construct, so we had to simplify again.

**Did testing early help us? | 早期测试对我们有帮助吗？**

We didn't test early enough, which was a mistake. When we finally tested our structure about halfway through, it collapsed immediately. If we had tested our base design earlier, we could have identified the stability issues and fixed them with time to spare.

## 🤝 Teamwork Lessons | 团队合作的经验

### What We Learned About Collaboration | 我们从协作中学到了什么

**What worked well in our team communication? | 我们团队沟通中什么做得好？**

We were good at sharing observations in real-time - when someone noticed the structure was leaning, we all stopped and discussed it. We also divided tasks clearly so everyone knew what to work on.

**What could we have done better as a team? | 作为一个团队，我们可以做得更好的是什么？**

We should have taken a moment to agree on a single plan before starting to build. We had multiple ideas going at once, which led to confusion. Also, we could have assigned a timekeeper to help manage our 18 minutes better.

**How did we handle disagreements? | 我们如何处理分歧？**

When we disagreed about design choices, we usually just tried both ideas quickly to see which worked better. This was somewhat effective but wasted time. We should have voted or had one person make a quick decision.

**Did we listen to everyone's ideas? | 我们有听取每个人的想法吗？**

Mostly yes, but Enkhjin and Avidkhuu's ideas tended to get more attention because they spoke up more confidently. Yebsira had some good suggestions that we didn't fully explore because we were already committed to another approach.

## 🔧 Engineering Thinking | 工程思维

**What would we change if we had 5 more minutes? | 如果我们还有 5 分钟，我们会改变什么？**

We would widen the base significantly and add more diagonal supports. We'd also reinforce all the marshmallow joints with extra spaghetti pieces. Most importantly, we'd test it multiple times and make adjustments based on what we learned.

**What engineering principles did we discover? | 我们发现了什么工程原理？**

*For example: triangles are stable, base needs to be wide, weight distribution, etc.*  
*例如：三角形是稳定的、底座需要宽、重量分布等*

We learned that a wider base provides more stability, and that triangles are indeed stronger than squares for support structures. We also discovered that the center of gravity matters - when the marshmallow on top was too heavy or off-center, the whole structure would tip. Weight distribution and balance are crucial.

## 🎓 Lessons Learned | 学到的经验

### Key Takeaways | 关键收获

**The most important thing I learned from this challenge | 我从这个挑战中学到的最重要的事情**

The most important lesson was that testing early and often is crucial. We could have saved so much time if we had tested our base design after just a few minutes instead of building the whole structure first. Failure is okay, but failing fast and learning from it is the key.

**One thing I would definitely do differently next time | 下次我肯定会做得不同的一件事**

I would insist on spending at least 5 minutes planning and agreeing on ONE design before anyone starts building. We wasted time because we were all working on slightly different ideas and had to keep adjusting.

**How does this challenge relate to real maker projects? | 这个挑战如何与真实的创客项目相关？**

*Think about: prototyping, iteration, teamwork, time constraints, resource limits*  
*思考：原型制作、迭代、团队合作、时间限制、资源限制*

This challenge perfectly mirrors real maker projects: we had limited resources (spaghetti and marshmallows), time constraints (18 minutes), and needed to prototype quickly. Just like in real projects, we had to iterate, work as a team, and make decisions under pressure. The challenge showed us that planning, testing, and adapting are all essential skills for any maker project.

## 🌟 Maker Mindset Connection | Maker 思维的联系

### How the Challenge Embodies Maker Principles | 挑战如何体现 Maker 原则

**Curiosity | 好奇**: How did we explore different possibilities?  
我们如何探索不同的可能性？

We tried different base shapes (triangle, square, four corners) and experimented with how to connect pieces. We were curious about whether longer or shorter spaghetti pieces would work better, and we tested both approaches.

**Hands-on | 动手**: How did building teach us more than just thinking?  
建造如何比仅仅思考教会我们更多？

We thought a tall, narrow structure would work, but actually building it showed us it was too unstable. The physical act of construction revealed problems we couldn't have predicted just by thinking - like how marshmallows compress under weight or how spaghetti bends.

**Fail Fast | 快速试错**: How many times did we fail and try again?  
我们失败了多少次并再次尝试？

We had at least 3-4 major collapses and rebuilds. Each time we learned something: first that our base was too narrow, then that our connections were too weak, then that we needed more support. Each failure taught us something new.

**Iteration | 迭代**: How did we improve our design step by step?  
我们如何一步步改进我们的设计？

We started with a simple triangle base, then added height. When that failed, we widened the base. When that still wasn't stable, we added diagonal supports. Each iteration incorporated lessons from the previous attempt.

**Interdisciplinary | 跨学科**: What different skills did our team bring?  
我们的团队带来了哪些不同的技能？

Enkhjin brought strategic thinking and planning skills. Avidkhuu had good spatial reasoning and could visualize structures. Yebsira was detail-oriented and good at precise connections. I (Nemuulen) contributed problem-solving and helped coordinate the team.

**Open Sharing | 开源分享**: Did we look at other teams' approaches?  
我们有观察其他团队的方法吗？

Yes, we glanced at other teams a few times, especially when we were stuck. We saw one team using a pyramid design and another using cross-bracing. We tried to incorporate some of these ideas, though we didn't have enough time to fully adapt them.
