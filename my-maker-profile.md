## 💡 My Project Idea | 我的项目想法

### Project Name (if you have one) | 项目名称（如果你有的话）

Solar Plant Companion

### What do I want to make? | 我想做什么？

*Describe your project idea in 3-5 sentences*  
*用 3-5 句话描述你的项目想法*

This project proposes a solar-powered, plant-like desk companion that merges sustainable design, environmental sensing, and AI-driven expression. Built from 3D-printed plastic, the device imitates natural plant behavior as it gently sways and leans toward brighter light, all fueled by solar energy. Integrated sensors track CO₂ levels, temperature, and such to provide insights about user’s environment. A lightweight AI model interprets these signals and generates expressive mood responses that reflect air quality and overall environmental comfort. By combining renewable energy, plant-inspired motion, and intelligent health-oriented sensing, the project aims to support user wellbeing.


### Why do I want to make this? | 为什么想做这个？

*What problem does it solve? What excites you about it?*  
*它解决什么问题？什么让你对它感到兴奋？*

I want to create this project because it addresses the growing concern about desk habits and environmental awareness in our daily workspaces. Many people spend hours at their desks without realizing the environment quality and their health. This project excites me because it combines multiple technologies I'm passionate about: renewable energy (solar power), environmental sensing, AI-driven behavior, and biomimetic design. The idea of creating something that not only monitors but also responds to its environment in a plant-like, organic way is fascinating. It's both functional and beautiful, serving as both a health monitor and an aesthetic desk companion that brings nature into indoor spaces.

## 🎯 My 6-Day Goals | 我的 6 天目标

### What does success look like for me at the end of this course? | 课程结束时，成功对我来说是什么样的？

Success for me means having a working prototype of my solar-powered plant companion that can sense environmental conditions, respond to light changes with movement, and display meaningful feedback about air quality. More importantly, I want to feel confident in my ability to troubleshoot problems, integrate different components, and continue building on what I've learned. Success also means having documented my journey well enough that others could understand and build upon my work.

### Specific goals for each day | 每天的具体目标

- **Day 1**: Complete maker profile, understand Arduino basics, set up development environment, and plan project architecture
- **Day 2**: Build basic circuit with sensors, learn to read sensor data, and start 3D design for the plant structure
- **Day 3**: Integrate servos for movement, program basic phototropic behavior, and refine 3D model
- **Day 4**: Add solar power system, implement sensor data processing, and begin AI model integration
- **Day 5**: Complete integration of all components, test full system, and debug any issues
- **Day 6**: Finalize documentation, create presentation, and prepare for showcase
